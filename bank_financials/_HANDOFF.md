## STANDING RULES

- **Quarterly only.** Never backfill missing quarters from annual data. Q4/year-end from a bank's own audited annual report is legitimate (it's the actual reported Dec-31 balance sheet); inventing Q1–Q3 from annual figures is not.
- **Prefer Solo/Standalone over Consolidated.** Explicitly flag when only Consolidated is available. Current split: 30 solo, 4 consolidated (SOUTHEASTB, ALARABANK, MTB — now *confirmed* not just inferred, BRACBANK), 1 solo-incl-OBU (NBL), 1 mixed basis break (NRBCBANK).
- **Prefer contemporaneous figures over later restated comparatives.** When a year's own report and a later report disagree, keep the earlier value and record the divergence verbatim in that year's `period_note` and in `sources.json`. Never silently reconcile or average. The one exception: if the contemporaneous figure is *arithmetically impossible* (doesn't reconcile with its own report's other rows), prefer the consistent later one and prove why in the note — see ISLAMIBANK FY2024 capital, or this session's SHAHJABANK FY2013 CAR / PREMIERBAN FY2024 reserves, for the pattern. UNIONBANK's FY2023 restatement is the *other* kind — the original figure was internally consistent, just later exposed as resting on manipulated data — that's a contemporaneous-vs-restated case, not an arithmetic-impossibility one, so both figures are kept, original preferred.
- **Delete downloaded PDFs after `pdftotext -layout`; keep and commit the `.txt`.**
- **Never `git add -A`.** Stage only `bank_financials/<BANK>/`, `annual_reports/<BANK>/*.txt`, and `bank_financials/_coverage_matrix.json`. This repo has many unrelated in-flight changes from other sessions.
- **Never force-push or `reset --hard` without asking.**
- Regenerate `_cross_bank.json`, `_benchmarks.json`, `_quality_scores.json` after adding or changing any bank — use the validated library at `~/.claude/projects/-Users-sukarnapaul-Downloads-DSE-Analysis/memory/cross_bank_regenerator.py` rather than re-deriving the formulas by hand (see [[reference-cross-bank-regenerator]] memory note for the non-obvious traps it already solved: `ldr_pct` scores descending, two different percentile formulas depending on which file, `change_pct` divides by `abs(prior)`, etc.). Re-run its self-test before trusting it — a hand-edit since the last run may have drifted it. Note: Q1-Q3 quarterly data does NOT feed into these 3 files — they're Q4/annual-only by design, so backfilling interim quarters never requires this regeneration step.
- If two agents' background sessions both touch a bank folder, one will lose. Have parallel sub-agents build/extend the bank's own files only and report back; do the shared-file regeneration and git commit centrally in one session, not per-agent — this is what avoided collisions across an 11-agent and a 9-agent parallel batch this session.
- **`git diff --cached --name-only` right before every commit is necessary but NOT sufficient** — this repo's shared index means another session's concurrent `git add`/`git rm --cached` can land in the gap between that check and the commit call itself (observed twice in one session). Prefer `git commit -m "..." -- <exact paths>` (pathspec AFTER the message, note the flag order) over a separate `git add` + bare `git commit` — it commits only the named paths' current content regardless of whatever else is sitting in the index, closing the race rather than narrowing it. After every commit, check its `--stat` output for any `delete mode` line you didn't expect, before pushing.
- **Never touch `.env`, `data/portfolios.json`, `data/research-notes.json`, `trades/trades_data/`, `watchlist/watchlist.json`, `data/trusted-devices.json`, `rgsnapshots/`, `cashledger/`, or `transactions/`, even if they appear "accidentally deleted."** These are mid-cleanup for privacy reasons (check `.gitignore`'s current *working-tree* content, not its commit history, if you need to confirm) — restoring them undoes someone else's deliberate work. See [[feedback-verify-staged-set-before-commit]].

## METHODOLOGY (established across all 36 banks)

1. Find the bank's investor-relations page yourself. A Wayback snapshot of the reports-listing page often reveals filenames no longer linked live. Check whether a prior session already downloaded/extracted text into `annual_reports/<BANK>/` before starting fresh — several banks this session turned out to already have most or all years' text sitting unused, turning what looked like a from-scratch research task into a pure extraction/parsing one.
2. `pdftotext -layout` each report. If a PDF extracts to ~0 lines it's a scan — don't retry with pdftotext, but OCR (`pdftoppm` + `tesseract`) is a legitimate last resort when no later report can recover that year instead (used this session for ICBIBANK FY2025, UCB FY2025, FIRSTSBANK FY2024, NRBCBANK FY2025 — always cross-validate the OCR'd figures against a second independent extraction of the same document, e.g. the auditor's prose narrative restating the same numbers, before trusting them).
3. Find the multi-year summary table. Titles vary wildly: "Five Year(s) Financial Summary", "Five Years at a Glance", "Key Financial Data and Indicators", "Five Years Performance", "MDB Financial Performance and Ratios", "Key Financial Indicators". Typically 3–4 overlapping tables reconstruct the full span. **Watch for a table that silently changes basis (Solo vs Consolidated) partway through a bank's own series without ever labelling itself** — found this session in PREMIERBAN (2015→2022 vintage) and STANDBANKL (its "Highlights" table is Consolidated throughout despite no label) — verify basis per-year, not once for the whole table.
4. **Parse every report, not just the ones you need**, and cross-check each figure against the same figure as republished elsewhere. This is how restatements get found automatically — IFIC had 85, ISLAMIBANK 83, ONEBANKPLC found a major IAS-8 restatement this way. A reusable parser with all the traps handled is saved at `~/.claude/projects/-Users-sukarnapaul-Downloads-DSE-Analysis/memory/bank_summary_table_parser.py`.
5. Write one JSON per year to `bank_financials/<BANK>/quarterly/<YEAR>_Q4.json`. Copy the schema from `bank_financials/UTTARABANK/quarterly/2025_Q4.json`. `basis` is `"reported"` by default; use `"calculated_from_assets_minus_equity"`, `"calculated_period_end_not_average"`, or `"calculated"` — and always describe the derivation in words too.
6. Validate every year: balance-sheet identity (assets = liabilities + equity), Tier1 + Tier2 = total capital, paid-up + reserves = equity, PBT − tax = PAT, income − expenditure = operating profit, and reported NPL% reproduced from classified loans ÷ loans. Flag anything over ~0.05%.
7. Write `sources.json` (per-source `source_priority`, `retrieval_date`, `status`, `used_for_years`, and a `note` for every discrepancy and gap — **always fill the top-level `reporting_basis` field itself**, not just prose in `notes`; two banks this session had the determination buried in notes and needed a follow-up fix), `ratios_timeseries.json`, and `insights.json` (`{bank_id, generated_at, methodology, risk_signals, historical_context, data_gaps}`). **Every risk_signal must cite specific values — never a vague claim.** Verify every number in `insights.json` programmatically against the JSON before committing; this has caught real errors (an ONEBANKPLC "uninterrupted asset growth" claim, an SBACBANK growth-rate typo).
8. Update `bank_financials/_coverage_matrix.json`. Use `"unavailable"` (not `"not_started"`) for years before a bank existed *and* for years confirmed permanently gone (bank merger/resolution) — reserve `"not_started"`/leave-as-is for a genuine publication lag.
9. Verify: read through the same JSON-loading logic `routes/bank-financials.js` uses (`readJSON` + sort), rather than only trusting a live `curl` — the server may require auth you don't have locally, but the route logic itself is simple enough to replicate in a one-off Node/Python script.
10. Commit only that bank's files plus the coverage matrix, with a message that states the year range, the sourcing decisions, and any data-quality findings.

## TRAPS THAT WILL COST YOU HOURS IF YOU DON'T KNOW THEM

**Extraction**
- Header rows with a trailing "% Changes in 2025 Over 2024" caption repeat year tokens — a duplicate-year check rejects the whole table.
- Typographic ligatures: `Proﬁt`, `oﬀ-balance` never match `profit`/`off-balance`. Fold them.
- Apostrophe variants `’ ‘ ʼ ´ \`` all appear in "Shareholders' Equity".
- Numbered row schedules (Islami Bank) lose their label text; parse by serial number — but verify the numbering is stable across report vintages first, it often shifts.
- **Vertical/horizontal analysis tables.** Many reports print the same rows as percentages of total assets or of a base year. Merging all tables in a report lets these overwrite real figures with `100`. Scope parsing to the real summary heading.
- **A bank's own "highlights"/"5-year" table can be a different basis than its labelled Balance Sheet**, with no indication in the table itself (PREMIERBAN, STANDBANKL — see methodology point 3).

**Silent data corruption**
- **Unit changes between reports.** NBL's FY2025 report switches to *crore* while every other year is millions. Always verify against an overlapping column.
- **Off-balance-sheet inflation.** When a later report's figure for an old year is ~25–30% above the earlier report's, check `total assets + off-balance-sheet items` before assuming a restatement. NRBCBANK's FY2016–18 columns are exactly this.
- **Wayback PDFs truncated at exactly 1,048,576 bytes** still report as "PDF document" to `file`. Verify with `pdfinfo`, and query the CDX API for the *largest* capture. This turned a 1 MiB stub of Rupali Bank's FY2019 report into the real 416-page, 50 MB file.
- **WAFs that return a fake HTTP 200.** southeastbank.com.bd serves a ~46KB HTML "Request Rejected" body instead of the PDF. rupalibank.com.bd and pubalibangla.com are behind a Cloudflare JS challenge (403 to everything). Always `file` the download.
- **Negative equity breaks ratios.** A loss ÷ negative equity is *positive*. ROE must be null when equity ≤ 0 — this bug had NBL scoring mid-pack on profitability while losing more than its entire capital base, and recurred independently in ICBIBANK's own `ratios_timeseries.json` this session (found and fixed across 11 years — the bank had a caveat *note* attached but never actually nulled the value). Check any new derived metric the same way — this session also found the same distortion could hit a YoY-growth-from-negative-base calculation (GIB), and a shrinking-RWA-denominator could make CAR look mechanically worse without capital actually deteriorating (ICBIBANK FY2025).
- **A bank going through active crisis/resolution may restate a recent "profit" into a "loss."** UNIONBANK's FY2023 is the clearest case in this dataset: originally an unqualified-opinion profit, later restated to a loss by the bank's own next report after Bangladesh Bank found manipulated data. This is a contemporaneous-vs-restated case (both figures were internally consistent on their own terms), not an arithmetic-impossibility one — keep the original, record both.

## WHAT THE DATA SHOWS (context for insights work)

FY2024 was a sector-wide asset-quality recognition event: median NPL went 5.48% → 9.55% and median CAR fell to 12.44% (measured on the original 27-bank set; medians have since shifted with 9 more banks added — recompute before quoting). **Reported profit is the least informative number in it.** Banks responded oppositely:

- **NBL** — balance-sheet insolvent, equity −24,003m, CAR −5.48%, NPL 59.69%.
- **IFIC** — provisioned, took a 25,617m loss, equity down to 0.96% of assets, CAR 1.82%.
- **ISLAMIBANK** — *cut* provisioning to 1,372m in the year classified investment grew 286,069m, and still reports a profit. Coverage 6.24%, leaving 884,350m unprovided = 12.7× equity.
- **ABBANK** — NPL 56.53%, CAR −3.78%.
- **ICBIBANK** — the most extreme in the whole dataset: 14 consecutive years of negative equity and negative CAR (not new in FY2024 — a sustained state since at least FY2012), CAR −291.87% and NPL 84.29% by FY2025.
- **The Sammilito-merger banks** (EXIMBANK, FIRSTSBANK, GIB, SIBL, UNIONBANK) didn't just report bad numbers — they stopped existing as independent reporting entities. FIRSTSBANK's FY2024 (CRAR 12-13%→4.92%, NPL 3.95%→91.75%, fraud findings) and UNIONBANK's FY2024 (equity −246bn after a restated FY2023) are the two most severe single-year collapses in this dataset.

Ranking on earnings alone would put Islami Bank *above* IFIC, and several of the FY2024/25-crisis banks *above* ICBIBANK. `unprovided_npl_x_equity` in `_cross_bank.json` is the metric that separates recognition from deferral, and `_quality_scores.json` is built around that deliberately — profit is one of five dimensions and hard-fail flags override the composite.

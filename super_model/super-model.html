Portfolio simulation — ৳1,000,000, buy-only

Every per-trade average on this page quietly assumes you could fund all simulated trades at once. This section drops that assumption: it replays the same 2-year trade streams — shipped plan and Explorer — through one simulated account with fixed starting capital and a cap on simultaneous positions, so the two plans finally compete for the same limited money the way they would in a real portfolio. Signals compete for free slots (strongest conviction wins), every fill pays 0.4% commission per side, and equity is marked to market daily against actual closes, which is what makes the drawdown figure real rather than a realized-only staircase. Sell signals are excluded throughout — DSE has no practical short mechanism, so a buy-only account is the honest frame. The grey dashed line is the do-nothing alternative: the DSEX index bought once and held.
